# Leader video: script (3 minutes)

> For leaders in the pilot branch, shown at the leaders' onboarding session and shared with them afterwards.
> Scenes 1 and 2 also work on their own for members on launch day. It follows the one-page guide (`leader-quick-start.md`) step for step.

## Before recording

- **Demo data only.** Record on the staging site with the demo ministry (`npm run db:seed:demo`), never production. No real names, phone numbers or journal answers may appear, including in the email inbox you show.
- **Phone screen, portrait.** Record a real mid-range Android phone with the system font at its default size, so leaders see exactly what they will see. Hide notifications first.
- **Language.** Record the voice-over in the language leaders use most (English, Filipino or Taglish). Keep the button names in English, as the app shows them, and put them in bold on-screen captions.
- **Captions.** Add captions to every scene (a subtitle file or burned in). Many people will watch it muted in a Messenger group, and some leaders are hard of hearing.
- **Pace.** The voice-over is about 355 words. Read calmly, that is about two and a half minutes; the scene times leave room for each tap to finish on screen before the next sentence.
- **Sharing.** Share it privately, in the pilot leaders' group or as an unlisted video, not on the ministry's public pages.
- **Re-check before recording.** The button names below are the app's wording on 2026-09-17. If the app changed, follow the app.

## Script

| # | Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:21 | GenTouch logo, then a leader's hands holding a phone. | Every day, people in our ministry spend quiet time with God and write down what He is teaching them. This portal helps you, as their leader, see who has shared, read what they wrote, and care for anyone who needs it. |
| 2 | 0:21–0:54 | A phone camera scans a leader's wallet card. **You're journaling with [leader]'s group.** Tap **I've journaled before**, type a mobile number and first name, answer the questions, tap **Send my journal**. **Thank you, [first name]! God bless you!** | Your members don't need an app or an account. They scan your QR card and tap "I've journaled before". That's everyone already in your group, even if they have only journaled on paper. "I'm new here" is for people new to the ministry. Then they answer the day's questions and tap "Send my journal". Their phone remembers them, so next time takes less than a minute. |
| 3 | 0:54–1:16 | Sign-in page: type an email, tap **Email me a sign-in link**, then **Check your email**. Switch to the mail app, open the email, tap the link. The Dashboard opens. | You don't need a password either. Enter your email, tap "Email me a sign-in link", then open the email on the same phone and tap the link. You'll stay signed in as long as you use the portal at least once a month. |
| 4 | 1:16–1:40 | **My account** → **Set up two-step verification**. The authenticator app: **Enter a setup key**, type the key. Back in the portal: the 6-digit code, **Turn on**. The backup codes are copied onto paper. | Journals are private, so there's one more step, and you only do it once. Open "My account" and set up two-step verification with Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator. Type the setup key into the app, enter the code it shows, and write your backup codes on paper. |
| 5 | 1:40–2:13 | Dashboard: **Daily Journal today: 9 of 12 received**. **Needs your attention: 4 journals waiting for review**. Open one, scroll the answers, choose **I'll follow up personally**, tap **Mark as reviewed**. | Each day, your Dashboard shows how many in your group have sent their journal. "Not yet" just means not yet: there's time until midnight, and late journals are welcome until nine the next morning. Under "Needs your attention", open a journal and read it prayerfully. If someone needs care, choose "I'll follow up personally" or "Ask a pastor to follow up". Then tap "Mark as reviewed". |
| 6 | 2:13–2:39 | **Follow-ups**: tap **I'm on it**, later **Mark resolved** with a short note. A member's profile: **Create personal link** → **Send by SMS**. Then **Add a pause**. | Your follow-ups wait in one list. Tap "I'm on it" when you reach out and "Mark resolved" when you're done. If someone can't scan your card, send them a personal link from their profile. And if someone is sick or travelling, add a pause, so no one worries about them. |
| 7 | 2:39–3:00 | A padlock over a journal. Then the quick-start guide, with the pilot coordinator's name and number. | One last thing: these journals are written to God and shared with you in trust. Please don't screenshot or forward them. Every step is in your one-page guide, and your pilot coordinator is ready to help. Thank you for shepherding your people. |

## Shot list for the recording session

1. Wallet card from **QR codes** → **Cards for a branch**, printed and cut. Use a demo leader's card.
2. A member's journal on a second phone that hasn't opened the journal before, so it starts at the welcome screen (scene 2).
3. A demo leader's sign-in email in a test inbox (scene 3).
4. Two-step verification on a demo leader account kept for recordings (scene 4). It can't be turned off from the portal, so don't use an account someone needs to use without an authenticator app.
5. Before scene 5, send three or four demo journals to the demo leader and leave them unreviewed, so the Dashboard has journals to review. The exact numbers on screen don't matter.
