# Leader quick start

> For leaders in the pilot branch. Fits on one A4 page. The ministry office fills in the two ✎ blanks before sharing it.

The portal shows whose journal has arrived, and lets you review your group's journals and care for anyone who needs a call or visit. Members need no account: they scan your QR card.

## 1. Sign in (no password)

1. On your own phone, open the portal address, enter your email and tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. Open the email **on the same phone** and tap the link (it works once, within 15 minutes). You stay signed in as long as you use the portal at least once every 30 days. "Too many attempts"? Wait a minute.

## 2. Turn on two-step verification (once)

Journal answers stay locked until you do.

1. Install **Google Authenticator** or **Microsoft Authenticator**, then in the portal go to **My account** → **Set up two-step verification**.
2. You can't scan your own screen, so in the app choose to **enter a setup key** and type the key shown. Enter the 6-digit code → **Turn on**.
3. Write the backup codes on paper and keep them safe. They let you in if you lose your phone.

## 3. Each day (about 10 minutes)

- **Daily Journal today** on the Dashboard shows who has sent theirs. By default a journal is on time until 11:59 PM, and late ones are accepted until 9:00 AM the next morning.
- **Needs your attention** lists journals to review, follow-ups with you and new registrations.
- **Review:** read the journal prayerfully and choose *No follow-up needed*, *I'll follow up personally* or *Ask a pastor to follow up*. Then tap **Mark as reviewed**. Only leaders see your note, never the member.
- **Follow-ups:** tap **I'm on it** when you start, then **Mark resolved** with a short note, or **Not needed**.

Primary Leaders see their whole branch's status. By default, only a person's direct leader reads their answers.

## 4. Helping your people

| When… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Someone is new to the ministry | They scan your card and tap **I'm new here**. Confirm them in **New registrations**. Everyone already in your group taps **I've journaled before**, even the first time. |
| A registration says **May already be in the directory** | It's probably someone in your group who tapped "I'm new here". Decline it and send them a personal link. |
| Someone can't scan or isn't recognised | Their profile → **Create personal link** → **Send by SMS** or copy it to Messenger. It works once, within 7 days. |
| Someone wants to journal without your card | After their first journal, add the page to their home screen (browser menu → **Add to Home screen**). On a shared phone, untick **Remember me on this phone** instead. |
| Someone shares their journal by phone call | Their profile → **Record a journal shared by phone**. |
| Someone is sick, travelling or on leave | Their profile → **Add a pause**. For one day, open that day → **Excuse this day**. |
| Your card is lost | It shows no one's information, so usually there's nothing to do. Tell the office if strangers register under your name. |

## 5. Keeping journals safe

- Your people wrote these journals to God and trusted you with them. Don't screenshot, forward or read them aloud.
- Keep a screen lock on your phone. If you ever see a journal from outside your group, tell the pilot coordinator right away.
